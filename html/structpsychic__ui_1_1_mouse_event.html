Detailed Description

What every mouse signal passes (mousePressed, clicked, mouseScrolled, contextMenuRequested...), also made of touches (source is Touch), pens and keys (a keyboard click or context menu, clickCount 0). Each div gets it in its own coordinates: the window fills it in window coordinates and every div on the way moves x and y into its own.
