Detailed Description

P2 Desk: Pivotal made into a desktop tool that is friendly with touch screens (.lavish/design-system-options.html, P2). Pivotal's colors (light on a white base), a denser type scale and controls, 4 px corners, flush panes. Its sizes switch with the input mode: for a pointer they are the board's "P2 Desk as it was"; by touch, approach B's (40 px rows and controls, text a step larger, icons 2 px larger), with approach C's hit slop as the floor.

S5 Float, the sample skin set's themes (.lavish/design-system-options.html, round 3): rounded panels floating over a dotted canvas, gentle two-layer shadows, neutral greys and a violet accent, light first. The canvas is the frame color; panels are the surface. Typography and metrics are shared by both.

Pivotal's design system (packages/ui/src/styles/tokens.css), translated value for value: P1 Faithful, the default skin set's look. Translucent tokens keep their alpha; Pivotal's color-mix values are precomputed. Typography and metrics are the defaults of their structs, which are Pivotal's.
