Detailed Description

P2 Desk: Pivotal as a dense desktop tool that is friendly with touch screens, with Desk Dark and Desk Light (themes/desk.hpp). Flush panes on hairlines, 4 px corners, 26 px rows, right-aligned inspector labels, value-field sliders, square swatches. Its sizes follow the input mode (the style manager's density): the board's desk sizes for a pointer, 40 px rows and controls by touch, and hit slop taking every target to 44 px.

Like Float, it falls back on PivotalSkins for every skin and rule, and only overrides the rules whose look differs (the slider's value field too: rules over RangeSkinBase's parts). 

window.skinSet<DeskSkins>();
