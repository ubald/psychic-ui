Detailed Description

An easing curve: a cubic Bézier from (0, 0) to (1, 1) through two control points, like CSS's cubic-bezier(). at(x) is how far along its way a value is (y) at a share of the duration (x, 0 to 1). y may leave 0-1 (an overshoot); the control points' x may not. 

Ease::Out                                            // A named curve, CSS's `ease-out`
Ease{.x1 = 0.16f, .y1 = 1.0f, .x2 = 0.3f, .y2 = 1.0f}  // Any other, as cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)
