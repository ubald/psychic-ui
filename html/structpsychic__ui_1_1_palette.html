Detailed Description

Color roles, not colors: a skin set maps them to its states and never picks a color itself. The roles are Pivotal's (packages/ui/src/styles/tokens.css): layers that separate by a step of grey, hairlines instead of dividers, three levels of text and one accent spent deliberately.
