Detailed Description

The motion of up to four channels toward a target, with a timing: along an easing curve for its duration, or pulled by a spring until it settles. Time is a monotonic clock's reading (Window::updateAnimations).

A retarget starts from the current value: a curve takes its whole duration again, a spring keeps its velocity.
